2. ROBLOX, DISCORD, AND EMAIL SIGN-IN

Roblox

Our Roblox authentication application requests the openid and profile scopes. These allow us to verify your Roblox identity and receive basic profile information, such as your Roblox user ID, username, display name, profile link, avatar, and account creation information returned by Roblox. Signing in can create a website account using your Roblox username. We store account identifiers and authentication/session information, including Roblox tokens, to operate your account and its connection.

You authorize access on Roblox's own authorization pages. These scopes do not give us your Roblox password, identity documents, or facial age-check images.

Discord

If you connect Discord, our website requests the identify scope to receive your Discord ID and username. We use that connection for an alternative sign-in method and connected community features. This website sign-in flow does not request your Discord email, address book, or friends list, and does not retain its Discord OAuth tokens.

Email and account linking

You may add a verified email as another way to sign in. We store the email address and send a short-lived verification code. Our email sign-in is passwordless. When you link login methods, they become associated with the same website account. You may also interact with community verification features that obtain Roblox/Discord associations through RoVer.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

  • Provide sign-in, account linking, profiles, account recovery through available login methods, and account administration.
  • Save and retrieve game data, models, builds, and other contributions; manage storage allowances and feature access.
  • Operate Eggbot commands, factions, verification roles, cosmetic rewards, donation displays, and connected community features.
  • Exchange relevant account-link, verification, faction, and earned-role information between the website and Eggbot so those features work together.

  • Send requested verification emails, answer support/privacy requests, and communicate relevant account or service information.
  • Detect errors and misuse, secure accounts and infrastructure, enforce our rules, handle disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Some features automatically assign verification or cosmetic roles, apply storage allowances, or limit actions based on account status, earned activity, and permissions. Contact us if you believe an account or access decision is incorrect.

5. PUBLIC INFORMATION AND ACCESS WITHIN THE SERVICES

Website profiles: public profiles can show your website username, creation date, roles, linked Roblox and Discord IDs and usernames, historical names and identity references, and faction name/tag. Previous identities and names can remain visible after unlinking. Email addresses and authentication tokens are excluded from the public profile response.

Eggbot and community features: identity-lookup commands can disclose Roblox-to-Discord associations in Discord channels. Approved faction details, membership information, and images can be displayed to other users. Donor messages and leaderboards can display Roblox usernames, Robux amounts, and linked Discord mentions. Consider this visibility before linking an account or using these features.

Game and moderation access: access to the live WoS viewer is controlled by assigned permissions. Users with the necessary permissions can view player names, virtual-world positions, and other game state made available to that viewer. Authorized staff can access relevant game, faction, account, support, and moderation information for their duties. These permissions do not make the live viewer a general public page.

Content you submit: faction images may be reviewed in a staff channel before approval. Selected music attachments can be uploaded as Roblox group audio assets. Content you publish or share through a game, Discord, or another platform may be accessible to its intended audience, and other people may retain copies.

7. COOKIES AND TECHNICAL INFORMATION

The website uses cookies to keep you signed in, protect sign-in flows, and remember the page you intended to visit. The session cookie, named sid, has a configured lifetime of approximately fourteen days. A redirect cookie supports login navigation. Session contents are stored on the server, and authentication records can have their own expiration or deletion rules.

You can use your browser settings to remove or block cookies. Doing so can sign you out or prevent login-dependent features from working. Browser deletion of a cookie does not itself delete account records held on our servers.

We process qualifying signed-in page visits and actions to award cosmetic roles. We also process request, browser, command, error, and security information for operation and troubleshooting. Our current Services do not use targeted advertising; the absence of targeted advertising does not eliminate this operational and reward-related processing.

9. RETENTION

We have not established fixed retention periods for all categories of information. Account records, linked-identity and username history, saved game data, contributions, faction records, donation records, and moderation records generally remain stored until removed through available controls or by an administrator. Unlinking a login method or ceasing to use a service does not automatically delete these records.

Some records have shorter technical lifetimes. Email sign-in codes are usable for up to ten minutes, and expired verification/rate-limit records are cleaned during subsequent code-request processing. Session cookies have the configured lifetime described above. Some temporary service records expire or are refreshed separately. An expired code, cookie, or cache entry does not mean your account or related gameplay information has been deleted.

Deleting a website account removes its records from the active website database, including stored account links/history, owned models, website roles, saved Roblox refresh tokens, and sessions. It does not automatically delete Eggbot or WoS records, external platform records, public messages or copies held by others, or backup copies.

We have not established uniform automatic deletion schedules for operational logs and backups, or a uniform completion period for manual deletion across all Services. Moderation, security, donation, dispute, or other records may need separate assessment where retaining information is required or permitted by applicable law. These current practices do not limit any deletion or retention obligations that applicable law imposes on us.

Contact tos-support@eggsdstudios.com to ask about retention or request access, correction, or deletion. We assess requests according to applicable law and the information and Services involved.

10. ACCOUNT CONTROLS AND PRIVACY REQUESTS

  • Manage linked accounts: use the website account controls to link or unlink supported login methods. Unlinking deactivates a connection while preserving history; it is a different action from account deletion.
  • Delete website information: use the separate website account-deletion flow. Available model controls also let you delete individual saved models or release model storage. These controls have the scope explained in section 9.
  • Revoke external authorization: use Roblox's or Discord's own settings to revoke an application's authorization. Unlinking on our website does not itself guarantee revocation at the external platform, and external revocation does not itself erase our stored records.
  • Request other changes: email tos-support@eggsdstudios.com for questions about public identity/history, Eggbot or WoS information, corrections, or deletion that website controls do not cover. Identify the relevant service and account, and describe your request. We may need to verify your control of the account or other authority to make the request. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to access information, obtain a copy, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to particular processing, withdraw consent for consent-based processing, or complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of prior processing. We handle requests within applicable legal requirements and will explain any applicable limits or additional verification needed.

Deleting content or an account may not remove copies already shared with other users or independently retained by another platform. Data-deletion obligations are assessed separately from any license to use a contribution under our Terms of Service.
